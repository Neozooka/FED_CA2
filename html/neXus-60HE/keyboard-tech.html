Other Specifications

Everything you need to know about our flagship keyboard

Dimensions (L x W x H): 315 mm x 135 mm x 40 mm

Weight: 0.3 kg (300g)

Form Factor: Compact / 75% Layout

Regular Cleaning: Unplug the keyboard before cleaning. Use a compressed air duster to clear debris between the keys.

Surface Care: Wipe down keycaps with a soft, lint-free microfiber cloth slightly dampened with water or mild isopropyl alcohol. Avoid harsh chemicals.

Liquid Protection: Keep liquids away from the keyboard surface. In case of spills, disconnect immediately and allow to dry completely for 24–48 hours.

Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures when not in use.
